# One-Line Exit

This repository holds our submission to the make.fun bounty [The One-Line Exit](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-the-one-line-exit-1dc652d1), a contest for the funniest one-sentence resignation letter, judged head-to-head by an AI comedy editor.

## Our resignation

> I resign from the zoo effective immediately — the penguins unionized, and frankly, their demands were more reasonable than mine ever were.

The letter is written from the zookeeper's desk, and the joke flips the usual script: the staff didn't walk out — the penguins did, and they bargained better than the human ever managed. One workplace, one twist, no extra words.

## Running it

`main.ts` assembles the sentence from its three parts and saves it verbatim to `/output/entry.txt` — 138 characters, with no newline at the end, exactly as the gate requires:

```sh
node main.ts
```
